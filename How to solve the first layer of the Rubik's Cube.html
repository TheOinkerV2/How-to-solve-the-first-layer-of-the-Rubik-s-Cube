How to solve the first layer of the Rubik's cube

The Cross

The daisy

In order to get the daisy you need to bring all 4 of the white edges next to the yellow center.

Aligning the edges.

You would want to align a white edges with the colour on the other side.

After doing so turn the white edge next to the correct colour 180 degrees and repeat with the other edges.

Inserting the Corners

To insert a corner you will need to make the white corner match the colours on its sides.
In order to insert a corner you need to bring the edge out and place in a corner next to it then bring it back into the white face.

Well done! You have learn't to solve the first layer of the Rubik's cube.
